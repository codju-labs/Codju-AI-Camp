How AI Builds Games

AI can write the code, but you are still the game designer.

Workflow

From Idea to Playable

1

Describe

Explain the player, goal, rules, controls, and style.

2

Generate

AI writes a first playable version.

3

Test

You play it and notice what feels confusing or boring.

4

Improve

Ask AI for changes, then test again.

Builder mindset

Do Not Ask for Everything at Once

AI

Small changes win

After the first version works, improve one thing at a time: controls, difficulty, art style, scoring, or win screen.

Good follow-up: Make the asteroids slower for the first 20 seconds, then speed them up gradually.
